Animated Workflow Examples

Three ways to show how an AI workflow runs. Press Play or Step on each one. All workflows and data are fictional examples.

1. A lead moves through the workflow

A dot follows one lead from form to CRM. Pick a high or low score to see the two paths.

Press Play or Step.

Use it for: product pages and demos, so a buyer sees each step and the approval point before paying.

2. How an AI agent loops until the job is done

An agent reads the task, picks a step, uses a tool, and checks the result. If the check fails, it tries again. The log shows each pass.

Agent log
Press Play to run the agent.
Task: find 3 local bakeries and write their websites to a sheet

Use it for: training and recipes, to explain why agents retry and where a check stops bad output.

3. One coordinator, several agents working at once

A coordinator splits the job and sends parts to three helpers at the same time. Each finishes at its own pace. The report is built when all three are done.

Press Play.

Use it for: showing clients how an AI team of agents and workers splits one job, the core idea of an agentic factory.
